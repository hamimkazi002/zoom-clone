HERE TO HELP

MeetSpace Help.

Everything you need to use your meeting confidently.

How do I create a meeting?

Choose Start meeting, sign in if needed, enter a password and create your room.

How do I join?

Enter the Meeting ID and password from your host. You may wait for approval.

Where are People and Chat?

Desktop: use the bottom toolbar to open the right panel. Phone: use the bottom sheet.

How do I change meeting time?

Hosts choose Adjust Time to add or reduce minutes, including a custom amount.
